About Us

Our Company

Our philosophy is to acquire existing single tenant and multi-tenant retail and restaurant properties throughout the United States. Our principal activities are the acquisition, ownership, and operation of free-standing net leased, single tenant retail properties.

Sam Librizzi has been involved in commercial real estate for more than 20 years. He formed WREI with the intent to purchase stabilized and distressed retail commercial real estate properties. Since inception, we have acquired over 260 commercial properties totaling over $360,000,000.

Individual properties range in size from as small as 1,200 square feet to as large as 200,000 square feet. In the aggregate, our properties contain over 500,000 square feet. The vast majority of our properties are single tenant retail properties. We also own land, subject to a ground lease, underlying both single tenant and multi-tenant retail properties.

Our properties are located in primary and secondary markets throughout the country. We are mainly buy and hold investors, but will consider selling immediately after closing should it make fiscal sense.

We offer our investors immediate income and increased long term income. Our dedicated team of real estate professionals and disciplined underwriting has enabled us to achieve consistent returns for our capital partners through a variety of market conditions.

We distinguish ourselves through our access to deals, focused investment strategy and proven expertise to deliver solid results. We seek investments that earn superior risk adjusted returns by identifying unique opportunities in select markets and capitalizing on highly structured transactions with attractive relative values to mitigate downside risk.
