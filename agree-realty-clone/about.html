About Us

A leading Real Estate Investment Trust focused on net leased retail properties

Who We Are

Our Story

Agree Realty Corporation is a fully integrated, self-administered, and self-managed real estate investment trust (REIT) focused on the acquisition and development of properties net leased to industry leading, omni-channel retail tenants throughout the United States.

As one of the largest net lease REITs in the country, we specialize in acquiring and developing properties that are leased on a long-term basis to industry-leading retailers. Our portfolio includes properties leased to top retailers across essential sectors including grocery, home improvement, convenience, and quick-service restaurants.

Our Heritage

Our History

Founded in 1971 as Agree Development Company, we have grown from a regional developer to a national leader in the net lease sector. Today, Agree Realty is a $12B+ industry leader with a portfolio spanning all 50 states.

Over five decades, we've built a reputation for excellence in the acquisition and development of retail properties. Our long-standing relationships with leading retailers, combined with our disciplined investment approach, have enabled us to grow into one of the largest net lease REITs in the country.

  • 1971 - Founded as Agree Development Company
  • 1994 - Initial Public Offering (NYSE: ADC)
  • 2010 - Began significant portfolio expansion
  • 2020 - Reached $10B+ in total market capitalization
  • Today - 2,600+ properties across all 50 states
Our Purpose

Our Mission

To be the premier net lease REIT, delivering consistent growth and value for our shareholders through disciplined acquisitions, strategic development, and operational excellence.
