What We Offer

Development Excellence

For over 50 years, Agree has been a preferred development partner for industry-leading retailers, priding ourselves on our ability to undertake all aspects of the development process from site identification to grand opening.

Our vertically integrated platform enables us to control all aspects of the development process, ensuring quality, efficiency, and successful outcomes for our retail partners.

How We Work

Our Process

Our experienced development team manages every phase of the development process, from initial site identification through construction and asset management.

Site Identification & Acquisition

Market feasibility and saturation evaluations to identify optimal locations that meet our retail partners' criteria and growth objectives.

Due Diligence & Entitlements

Comprehensive due diligence including environmental assessments, title review, and zoning analysis. We manage all entitlement processes and regulatory approvals.

Design & Engineering

Full architectural and engineering services coordinated with tenant specifications. We ensure designs meet local codes while optimizing functionality and aesthetics.

Permitting

Complete permit acquisition and management of the approval process with local municipalities. Our team has deep experience navigating complex permitting requirements.

Construction Management

Ground-up construction with rigorous quality control, timeline management, and budget oversight. We coordinate all aspects of construction to deliver on time and on budget.

Asset Management

Ongoing property management and lease administration. We maintain our properties to the highest standards, ensuring long-term value for both our company and our tenants.

Our Capabilities

Development Services

Build-to-Suit Development

We specialize in build-to-suit developments for industry-leading retailers. Our team works closely with tenants to deliver properties that meet their exact specifications, from site selection through construction and lease-up.

  • Custom design and specifications
  • Single-tenant retail buildings
  • Multi-tenant retail centers
  • Ground lease structures available

Redevelopment

We also undertake redevelopment projects, transforming existing properties to meet the evolving needs of modern retailers. Our redevelopment expertise includes:

  • Repositioning and adaptive reuse
  • Façade improvements and modernization
  • Parking and site improvements
  • Building expansions and modifications
